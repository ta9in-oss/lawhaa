Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28

This policy explains what data Lawhaa (lahwaa.ta9in.com) collects and how it's used.

Sign-in with Google

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile identifier from Google via our authentication provider (InstantDB). We use this only to identify your account and associate it with your boards.

Board data

Boards, drawings, and related metadata (titles, timestamps, share tokens) you create are stored to provide sync, persistence, and sharing features. We do not sell this data or share it with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (hosting, database).

Local storage

When not signed in, boards may be stored only in your browser's local storage and not sent to our servers.

Data retention

Data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete boards at any time; deleting your account removes associated data.

Third parties

We use Google (OAuth sign-in), InstantDB (database and auth), and Cloudflare (hosting) to operate the service. Each handles data under their own privacy terms.

Contact

Questions about this policy or to request data deletion: support@ta9in.com
